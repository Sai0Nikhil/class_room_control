Abstract
  • Overview: Integrated embedded automation framework engineered for educational institutions.
  • Problem: Severe screen glare, note-taking blackout dilemma, and empty classroom energy waste.
  • Methodology: Dual-zone dimming (0% Front, 20% Back), motorized blind servo with warning chime, and PIR 30s Eco-Sleep.
  • Outcome: 48.6% power savings and 100% specular glare eradication.
3
Introduction
  • Background: Modern classrooms rely on high-lumen digital projectors and smart displays.
  • Problem: Sunlight glare degrades projection; turning all lights off prevents note-taking.
  • Motivation: Optimize visual ergonomics, student attention, and campus energy conservation.
  • Scope: Universities, conference centers, and auditoriums.

Hardware Design & Pinout Map
  • PA0 (A0): LDR Light Sensor (ADC1_IN0, 12-bit analog input)
  • PA1 (A1): LM35 Temperature Sensor (ADC1_IN1, 10 mV/°C linear analog)
  • PA4 (A2): PIR Motion Sensor (Digital input, high on occupant presence)
  • PB4 (D5): Manual Override Switch (Pull-up, force presentation mode)
  • PA6 (D12): SG90 Servo Motor (TIM3_CH1 PWM, 50 Hz, 0° to 180° blinds)
  • PA7 (D11): Piezo Buzzer (Pre-motion warning audio chime)
  • PB0, PB1, PB2: Relays for Front Light (PB0), Mid Light (PB1), Back Light (PB2)
  • PB5 (D4): Exhaust Fan Relay (Thermal closed-loop trigger @ >28°C)
  • PB8, PB9 (D15, D14): I2C1 SCL/SDA for 16x2 HD44780 LCD Display (0x27)
7
Software Design (FSM)
  • STATE_DAYLIGHT: LDR > Threshold -> Lights OFF, Blinds 0° OPEN.
  • STATE_DARK: LDR < Threshold -> All 3 Lighting Zones 100% ON.
  • STATE_PRESENTATION: Front Light 0% OFF, Back Lights 20% Dimmed, Blinds 180° CLOSED with warning chime.
  • STATE_ECO_SLEEP: No PIR motion for 30s -> All loads disconnected (<2W).

Implementation Workflow
  • 1. Initialize Nucleo-F446RE project in STM32CubeIDE.
  • 2. Configure GPIOs, ADC1 (Ch 0, 1), TIM3 PWM, I2C1, USART2.
  • 3. Generate HAL initialization drivers & link math lib.
  • 4. Implement FSM control loop & 8-point oversampling.
  • 5. Build binary with arm-none-eabi-gcc toolchain.
  • 6. Flash binary via ST-LINK V2 debugger.
  • 7. Validate dual-zone dimming & live CSV telemetry.

Experimental Setup
